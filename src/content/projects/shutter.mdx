When an HL7 delivery fails in a hospital, the usual fix is a resend from the Message Viewer. If the patient has moved
since, that resend moves them back. Shutter turns each failed delivery into a case instead.

## How a case runs

1. **Evidence, sealed.** One immutable snapshot of the message, the receiving system's state and history, and every
   other operation for the encounter. Patient identifiers become per-case tokens before anything reaches a model.
2. **A board of agents proposes.** Three specialists (conformance, encounter, impact) review the evidence in parallel
   on IRIS AI Hub, and a coordinator writes one plan, recording every specialist it overruled and why.
3. **A deterministic gate checks.** Identity, ordering, state transition, authorisation, concurrency, freshness of the
   evidence and the receiver's capabilities. An unknown blocks a write as surely as a failed check.
4. **A person releases it,** bound to the exact plan, or the site's written policy does for the cases it settles.
5. **One conditional write, then verification** in the receiver's own history. "Acknowledged" and "Verified" are never
   the same label.

The agents propose; the gate disposes.
