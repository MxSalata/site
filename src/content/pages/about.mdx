I connect the systems of NHS hospitals, councils and clinics so that the people caring for patients have the information they need.

Today I'm a Senior Customer Deployment Specialist at Blackford, which orchestrates AI across the healthcare enterprise:
one platform that gives hospitals access to more than 130 AI applications from over 50 vendors. I lead deployments of
the platform and its applications, on site or in the cloud, integrating them with each hospital's PACS, RIS and EMR so
that AI results arrive in the systems clinicians already use.

I've worked in healthcare integration since 2019, when I moved from sterile services at Luton and Dunstable University
Hospital into its IT integration team. Since then I've built integrations in-house at Luton and Dunstable and
Bedfordshire Hospitals, and at Synanetics, where I architected and delivered integrations for six of the more than 20
NHS trusts it supports.

Patient administration is where I'm most at home: inpatient and outpatient workflows, from referral and appointment to
admission, transfer and discharge, and the feeds that carry them to every system downstream.

## Work I'm proud of

- **ePhlebotomy at Warrington and Halton:** interfaces linking GP orders in MOLIS, WASP's online booking and the Lorenzo
  PAS, with NHS Spine lookups, so patients book and manage their own blood tests.
- **DrDoctor at Warrington and Halton:** HL7 integration for the patient engagement portal, with bespoke
  transformations, subscription tables, exclusion lists and data warehouse jobs.
- **DocAssure:** as sole developer and architect, I built Synanetics' document management framework for Sheffield
  Children's Hospital. It routes every clinical document, including to GP practices over NHS MESH, and tracks each
  delivery.
- **Social care data for the Leicester, Leicestershire and Rutland Care Record:** a Node.js pipeline that turns council
  records into FHIR for Interweave.
- **[Aperture](/projects/aperture/):** a management portal for InterSystems IRIS. It came 2nd of 37 entries in the
  Experts nomination of InterSystems' [“Build Your Own Management Portal”
  contest](https://openexchange.intersystems.com/contest/48), and 1st in its Freshmen nomination.

## Sterile services

From 2018 to 2020 I worked in the sterile services department at Luton and Dunstable University Hospital,
decontaminating and sterilising surgical equipment and endoscopes. After I joined the IT integration team in 2019, I
kept it on as a second job. I was certified as a Flexible Endoscope Reprocessor (CFER).
